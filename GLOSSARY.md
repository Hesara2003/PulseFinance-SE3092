# PulseFinance

Personal finance for a Sri Lankan earner with irregular, multi-currency, multi-source income who wants one savings goal to feel real.

## Income

**Income Source**:
One of the four channels money arrives through: Salary, Freelance, AdSense, Crypto.
_Avoid_: Stream type, channel

**Realised Income**:
Money that has actually landed, counted in the month it arrived, at the LKR value actually received.
_Avoid_: Cleared cash, liquid cash, earned

**Pending Invoice**:
Freelance money billed to a client but not yet received; never treated as spendable.
_Avoid_: Pipeline, expected income

**Overdue Invoice**:
A Pending Invoice whose due date has passed without being marked received.
_Avoid_: Late payment

**Crypto Trading Result**:
The realised profit (positive) or loss (negative) recorded when a crypto trade is closed; holdings are not tracked.
_Avoid_: Crypto income, yield, portfolio value

**Crypto YTD Net**:
The sum of all Crypto Trading Results since 1 January of the current year.
_Avoid_: Crypto earnings, crypto balance

**Income Basis**:
The monthly income figure the Safe-to-Spend Allowance is built on: this month's Realised Income, or the three-month average if higher, with crypto profits left out of the average but crypto losses kept in.
_Avoid_: Expected income, estimated income

**Gross Salary**:
The salary before deductions, as written on the payslip.
_Avoid_: Salary (unqualified), CTC

**Take-Home Salary**:
Gross Salary minus employee EPF (8%) and APIT; the amount that actually reaches the bank on the 25th.
_Avoid_: Net pay, salary credited

**EPF Balance**:
Kavindu's accumulated Employees' Provident Fund contributions (his 8% plus the employer's 12%) — savings he owns but cannot spend before retirement.
_Avoid_: Pension, retirement savings

## Spending

**Committed Expense**:
An expense Kavindu has to pay regardless of choice (rent, utilities, auto-debits); the category proposes it, each expense decides.
_Avoid_: Fixed cost, bill, essential

**Discretionary Expense**:
An expense Kavindu chose to make and could have skipped; the complement of a Committed Expense.
_Avoid_: Lifestyle spend, variable cost, wants

**Safe-to-Spend Allowance**:
The LKR amount per day Kavindu can spend on Discretionary Expenses for the rest of the month without missing rent or his goal saving.
_Avoid_: Budget, balance, liquidity

**Subscription Risk**:
How likely a recurring charge is a forgotten "zombie": High, Medium or Low, judged from Kavindu's charge-day answers and, for app subscriptions, recent app usage.
_Avoid_: Leak score, unused flag

**Seasonal Peak**:
A month where Kavindu's spending is predictably above normal — Avurudu, Vesak, Poson, December, or a month his own history shows ≥ 20% above average.
_Avoid_: Festival budget, high season

## Habits

**Spending Freeze**:
A self-imposed promise not to spend on one account until a chosen date; the app reminds him when he logs against it but never blocks the record.
_Avoid_: Card lock, card block, frozen card

**Freeze Break**:
An expense logged against an account while its Spending Freeze is active.
_Avoid_: Violation, failure

**Logging Gap**:
A stretch of days with no records; treated as missing data, never as zero spending or as failure.
_Avoid_: Broken streak, missed day
